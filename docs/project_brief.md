# Assessment brief (summary)

**Module:** R Programming for Data Science (ITRDA3-11), BSc Information Technology (Data Science), Eduvos, NQF Level 7.

**Scenario:** analyse the Stack Overflow Developer Survey (2021-2023) to find out which programming languages and databases users work with and want to use, so that Stack Overflow can improve its forums and learning material. Only nine of the 80+ columns are used: `YearsCode`, `MainBranch`, `Country`, `EdLevel`, `LanguageHaveWorkedWith`, `LanguageWantToWorkWith`, `DatabaseHaveWorkedWith`, `DatabaseWantToWorkWith` and `Age`.

| Question | Task |
|---|---|
| 1 | Read and bind the three datasets with a year indicator; standardise country names; treat missing values and choose a small set of countries, justifying both choices |
| 2 | Categorise `MainBranch` and `EdLevel`; analyse the yearly trend of participants per branch; visualise the relationship between branch and age, country, education and years of coding |
| 3 | Find the top 5 databases and languages respondents use and want to use (multi-select answers split on ";"); compare the top 5 databases over the three years |
| 4 | Visualise the trend of the top 5 languages and the relationship between years of coding and the top languages and databases; write a short report with insights and actionable recommendations |

The full brief is the property of Eduvos.
